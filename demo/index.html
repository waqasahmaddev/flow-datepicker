flow-datepicker

Date, time and date range picker for plain HTML forms. Every example below is a real <input>.

Date range

Pill trigger, presets and two months. A wrapper with two date inputs.

Date range that stays open

With data-fdp-close-on-select="false" the calendar stays open after a pick. Click outside or press Escape to close.

Date range with Custom

With data-fdp-calendar="custom" only the presets show. Pick "Custom range" to open the calendar.

Date range in a normal field

Same range picker with data-fdp-trigger="field": a read-only field instead of the pill.

Date

Field trigger and one month.

Date with limits

Native min, max and required. Days outside the range are dimmed.

Date in another language

Names, order and week start come from lang.

Time

One time row. Hover a part for the up and down handles, or use the arrow keys, the mouse wheel or type digits. Minutes step by 5 (step="300").

Date and time

One month with the time row below it.

Form post

The server echoes exactly what the native inputs post.

htmx swap

Content swapped in by htmx starts by itself.
