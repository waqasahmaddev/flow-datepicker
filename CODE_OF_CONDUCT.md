# Code of conduct

This project is open to everyone who wants to use it or help improve it.

## Be kind

- Be respectful and patient, in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you judge.
- Give feedback on the code, not on the person.
- Keep it on topic and keep it constructive.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam and self promotion unrelated to the project.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep doing so.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Security problems go through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
